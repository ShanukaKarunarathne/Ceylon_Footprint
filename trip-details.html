Trip Overview

Experience the ultimate romantic getaway in Paris, the City of Lights. This carefully crafted 7-day journey takes you through the most iconic landmarks, hidden gems, and romantic spots that make Paris the world's most beloved destination for couples.

From sunrise breakfast at charming cafés to sunset cruises along the Seine, every moment of your trip is designed to create unforgettable memories with your loved one.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

01

Arrival & Welcome

Morning: Arrival at Charles de Gaulle Airport, private transfer to hotel

Afternoon: Check-in, welcome champagne, rest and explore hotel surroundings

Evening: Welcome dinner at hotel's rooftop restaurant with Eiffel Tower views

02

Iconic Paris

Morning: Visit Eiffel Tower with skip-the-line access, photo session

Afternoon: Seine River cruise with lunch, explore Île de la Cité

Evening: Romantic dinner at Le Jules Verne restaurant

03

Art & Culture

Morning: Private guided tour of the Louvre Museum

Afternoon: Stroll through Tuileries Garden, visit Place Vendôme for shopping

Evening: Free time, optional opera or theater show

04

Versailles Day Trip

Morning: Private transportation to Palace of Versailles

Afternoon: Guided tour of palace and gardens, lunch at Versailles

Evening: Return to Paris, dinner at traditional French bistro

05

Montmartre & Romance

Morning: Explore Montmartre district, visit Sacré-Cœur Basilica

Afternoon: Artist square visit, portrait drawing session

Evening: Romantic dinner with panoramic city views

06

Shopping & Relaxation

Morning: Shopping on Champs-Élysées and luxury boutiques

Afternoon: Spa treatment at hotel, wine tasting experience

Evening: Farewell dinner cruise on the Seine

07

Departure

Morning: Check-out, last-minute souvenir shopping

Afternoon: Private transfer to airport for departure
